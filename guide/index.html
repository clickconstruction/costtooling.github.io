Your first day

Thirteen short steps, from opening the app to sending the owner one page. Try them on the sample job first.

  1. 1

    Open costtooling.com on your phone. Tap Open the app. Then tap Let's go.

    Not sure where things are? Tap Show me around instead. The app walks you through every screen on the sample job.

    On an iPhone, the card also shows how to put the app on your home screen. Do that first.

  2. 2

    Tap the job to open it. There is a sample job to practise on.

    Stuck on any screen? Tap the yellow Help button at the top. Then tap How do I… to come back to this page.

  3. 3

    The job opens on four steps. Do them in order each time you visit the job. Tap Take photos.

  4. 4

    Tap Take a photo and take the picture. Or tap Pick photos to use ones you were texted.

  5. 5

    Under Which part of the job is it? tap Pick a part… Then pick the part the photo shows.

    A part is one piece of the work, like Framing or Roofing.

  6. 6

    Write a note if you like. Tap Save.

    The app opens that part and shows your photo under it.

    More details holds the weather, the people on site and the date. You can skip it.

  7. 7

    Tap Parts at the bottom. Tap a part.

    Each part shows how far along it is and how many photos it has.

  8. 8

    Tap the words that fit what you saw: Not started, Just started, About half, Nearly done or Done.

    It saves as soon as you tap. Changed your mind? Tap other words.

    Want a number? Tap Type an exact percent.

  9. 9

    Got a bill from the contractor? Tap Bill at the bottom. The picture shows which column to find on the bill. It is marked %.

    On most bills the column is called % (G ÷ C).

    No bill yet? Skip this step and the next one. Nothing else changes.

  10. 10

    Type that number in the box beside each part. Leave a part empty until the contractor bills for it.

    Where the bill says more than you saw, the app tells you. The job's first screen says it too. Tap See which part there.

    When a new bill comes, type over the old numbers.

  11. 11

    Tap Send at the bottom. This is the page the owner gets. Look it over. Then tap Choose how to send it. Nothing goes until you pick.

    Want one picture instead? Scroll down and tap Make a picture to text instead.

  12. 12

    Tap Text it or send it. Your phone's share menu opens. Pick who gets it.

    You can also tap Save it as a picture, Print it or Email the numbers.

  13. 13

    Your jobs are saved on this phone. Want them on another phone too? You need an account from your company. On the job list, below the jobs, tap I have an account or I have a code. The next sections show how.

    To get to the job list, tap Home at the bottom, then Jobs at the top.

    Got a code from your company? Tap I have a code instead.

    Not ready? Everything still works on this one phone.

Add someone to your team

For the person who runs the company. There are three ways: by email, with a one-time code, or with one code for the whole team.

  1. 1

    Open costtooling.com. On the job list, tap the cloud button at the top, beside Help.

  2. 2

    Type your email and password. Tap Sign in.

  3. 3

    First time only: type your company name. Tap Create company.

  4. 4

    Tap the cloud button again. The green dot means your jobs are saved to the cloud.

  5. 5

    Tap Team.

  6. 6

    Way 1 · By emailType their email. Tap Invite. They get an email with a link.

    Change Member to Admin if they should be able to add people too.

  7. 7

    Way 2 · One-time codeLeave the email box empty. Tap Invite.

  8. 8

    Read the code out or text it. It works for one person, for 7 days.

  9. 9

    The code waits under Pending invites. Tap the × to cancel it.

  10. 10

    Way 3 · Whole teamTap Create join code.

  11. 11

    Share this code with everyone. It works for up to 25 people, for 30 days.

  12. 12

    Regenerate makes a new code and stops the old one at once.

  13. 13

    Stop turns the code off.

Join with a code

For a new person. You need a code from whoever runs your company.

  1. 1

    Open costtooling.com. Tap the cloud button at the top. Then tap Have an invite code?

    Or tap I have a code on the job list. It opens the same screen.

  2. 2

    Type the code. Wait for the green line. It names your company.

  3. 3

    Type your email. Tap Continue.

  4. 4

    Open your email. Tap the link. Choose a password. Tap Save and continue.

    Already have a CostTooling account? Then the code adds you to the company right away. Just sign in.

Forgot your password

You can fix this yourself. It takes one email.

  1. 1

    On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password?

  2. 2

    Type your email. Tap Send reset link.

  3. 3

    Check your email. The link works for one hour.

  4. 4

    Tap the link. Choose a new password. Tap Save and continue.

  5. 5

    No password at all? Type your email. Then tap Email me a sign-in link. No password needed.

  6. 6

    Want a new password any time? Tap the cloud button, then Change password.
