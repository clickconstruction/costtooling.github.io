How it works

STEP 1

Walk the job

Take a photo and pick the part of the job it belongs to. Add a note if you like.

STEP 2

Mark what you saw

Tap the words that fit each part of the job, like about half or nearly done. Type what the contractor billed beside it.

STEP 3

Send one page

The owner update builds itself. Share it by text, email or print.
