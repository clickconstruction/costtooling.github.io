Your first day

Nine taps from opening the app to sending a report. Try them on the sample job first.

  1. 1

    Open costtooling.com on your phone. Tap Let's go.

    Not sure where things are? Tap Show me around instead. The app walks you through every screen on the sample job.

    On an iPhone, the card also shows how to put the app on your home screen. Do that first.

  2. 2

    Tap the job to open it. There is a sample job to practise on.

  3. 3

    Tap Add photo.

  4. 4

    Tap Camera and take the picture. Or tap Photos to pick one you already have.

  5. 5

    Pick the Line item the photo is for, so it shows under that item in the log. Write a short note if you like. Tap Save entry.

  6. 6

    Tap Progress at the bottom. Drag the slider for the work that got done. Or tap a percent.

    Every number on every screen follows the slider.

  7. 7

    Tap Report at the bottom. Tap the share button in the top corner.

  8. 8

    Pick how to send it: Share…, Save image, Print / PDF or Email.

  9. 9

    Want your jobs on every phone? Tap Sign in at the top of the job list. The next section shows how.

    Not ready? Everything still works on this one phone.

Add someone to your team

For the person who runs the company. There are three ways: by email, with a one-time code, or with a team code for the whole crew.

  1. 1

    Open costtooling.com. Tap the account button in the top corner.

  2. 2

    Type your email and password. Tap Sign in.

  3. 3

    First time only: type your company name. Tap Create company.

  4. 4

    Tap the account button again. The green dot means your jobs are saved to the cloud.

  5. 5

    Tap Team.

  6. 6

    Way 1 · By emailType their email. Tap Invite. They get an email with a link.

    Pick Admin in the dropdown if they should be able to add people too.

  7. 7

    Way 2 · One-time codeLeave the email box empty. Tap Invite.

  8. 8

    Read the code out or text it. It works for one person, for 7 days.

  9. 9

    The code waits under Pending invites. Tap the × to cancel it.

  10. 10

    Way 3 · Whole crewTap Create join code.

  11. 11

    Share this code with everyone. It works for up to 25 people, for 30 days.

  12. 12

    Regenerate makes a new code and stops the old one at once.

  13. 13

    Stop turns the code off.

Join with a code

For a new person. You need a code from whoever runs your company.

  1. 1

    Open costtooling.com. Tap the account button. Then tap Have an invite code?

  2. 2

    Type the code. Wait for the green line. It names your company.

  3. 3

    Type your email. Tap Continue.

  4. 4

    Open your email. Tap the link. Choose a password. Tap Save and continue.

    Already have a CostTooling account? Then the code adds you to the company right away. Just sign in.

Forgot your password

You can fix this yourself. It takes one email.

  1. 1

    On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password?

  2. 2

    Type your email. Tap Send reset link.

  3. 3

    Check your email. The link works for one hour.

  4. 4

    Tap the link. Choose a new password. Tap Save and continue.

  5. 5

    No password at all? Tap Email me a sign-in link instead. No password needed.

  6. 6

    Want a new password any time? Tap the account button, then Change password.
