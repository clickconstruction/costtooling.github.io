CostTooling

A phone app for tracking a job from the site: photos, progress, costs, schedule, and the one-page report that comes out of them. This is an early working version with one sample job. Open it on your phone for the real thing; it works here with a mouse too.

Everything you add stays on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you share it. "Back up my data" in the bulb menu saves a copy.

How to help: use it like you would on a job. Whenever you think "I wish it did…", tap the yellow bulb and write it down. "Email all ideas" sends them to us.

See every screen at a glance →

What works

  • Your own projects, with line items from a template or from scratch.
  • Photos from the camera or the camera roll, with a note, conditions and the line item they belong to.
  • Progress sliders and logged costs per line item; every number updates everywhere.
  • Cover page from your photos, saved to Photos or shared.
  • Schedule view and a one-page report you can share, save or print.
Things we'd love your take on
  1. Is percent complete per line item how you think about progress, or is it tasks and dollars separately?
  2. Should "cost so far" come from receipts you log, or is earned value enough?
  3. Which photos belong on a cover page: the latest, or ones you pick?
  4. Who sees your reports most often, and what do they ask you?
