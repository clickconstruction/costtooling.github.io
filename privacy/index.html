Privacy

Last changed November 25, 2026.

The short version

  • You can use CostTooling without an account. Then your jobs and photos stay on your own phone or computer, and nothing is sent to us.
  • If you sign in, your jobs and photos are also saved to our cloud storage, so you can see them on another phone and your company's team can see them too.
  • We do not sell your information, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools.

Who runs CostTooling

CostTooling is run by Click Construction. Write to us at help@clickconstruction.com about anything on this page.

If you do not sign in

Everything you add stays in the browser on your device: your jobs, the parts of each job, the bill figures you type, your notes and your photos. We cannot see it and it is not copied anywhere. Clearing your browser's data, or using Start over under Help, removes it.

The name you type on the Send tab, and any ideas you save under Tell us what is confusing, also stay on your device. Ideas are sent to us only if you press Email all ideas, which opens your own email app with a message you can read before you send it. That message includes a short list of recent technical events in the app, such as an error message.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. When you are signed in we keep:

  • Your account: your email address, and a password that is stored scrambled so that nobody, including us, can read it.
  • If you use Continue with Google: Google tells us your name, your email address and your profile picture. We use them only to sign you in and to show which account is yours. We do not see your Google password, and we do not ask Google for anything else, such as your email, contacts, calendar or files.
  • Your company: its name, who belongs to it, and the invitations and join codes it has made.
  • Your jobs and photos: a copy of each job on your device, with its photos. People who belong to the same company in CostTooling can see that company's jobs and photos. Nobody else who uses CostTooling can. We look at stored jobs and photos only when we need to, to fix a problem or because you asked us to.

What you send to others

The owner update and the picture to text are made on your device. They go only where you send them, through your own text, email or print.

Who helps us run it

We use a few other companies to run CostTooling. They handle information only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase stores accounts, jobs and photos for people who sign in.
  • Google signs you in, if you choose Continue with Google.
  • Resend delivers our sign-in, password and invitation emails.
  • GitHub hosts this website, Cloudflare directs visitors to it, and jsDelivr delivers one piece of the app's code. Like any website's hosts, they see the internet address of the device that asks for a page.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it with anyone else unless the law requires us to.

Cookies and tracking

CostTooling does not use advertising or analytics cookies and has no tracking tools. The app keeps your work, and the fact that you are signed in, in your browser's own storage on your device.

Keeping and deleting

What is on your device stays until you remove it. What is in our cloud storage stays while your account is open. Deleting a job in the app removes it from our cloud storage as well; some of its photo files can remain there until we clear them.

To have your account, your jobs and your photos deleted from our cloud storage, or to get a copy of them, write to help@clickconstruction.com from the email address on your account. To stop Google sign-in from working with CostTooling, you can also remove CostTooling in your Google Account, under Security, in the list of apps with access.

Children

CostTooling is a work tool for adults. It is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly keep their information.

Changes to this page

If we change what we keep or how we use it, we will change this page and the date at the top.
